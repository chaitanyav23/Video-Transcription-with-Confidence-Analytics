# Phase 10 Inferences

## Scientific Findings
1.  **Granularity is Paramount:** The most mathematically sound models will fail if their outputs are aggregated too aggressively. Speaker attribution must happen at the smallest possible linguistic unit (the Word) and remain there.
2.  **Oracle Nullifies Enhancement:** If a diarization model is provided with perfect (Oracle) speech boundaries, it prefers raw, unadulterated audio for extracting speaker embeddings. Enhancement (like DFN) is only useful if the model has to *find* the speech itself.

## Engineering Findings
1.  **Diagnostic Metadata is Invaluable:** The `bwc_word_metadata.json` artifact generated by the custom fusion script was the only reason the team could diagnose the "Majority Vote" flaw. Without it, the cpWER regression would have been a mystery.
2.  **Dual-Branch Complexity:** Managing two separate streams of audio increases the likelihood of temporal misalignment. The fusion script must be robust against small variations in sampling alignment.

## Architectural Bottlenecks
*   **The Fusion Layer:** We have solved the Audio problem (Raw is better), the ASR problem (WhisperX is robust), the VAD problem (Pyannote High-Recall), and the Diarization problem (Oracle MSDD). The only remaining bottleneck is accurately merging these outputs.
